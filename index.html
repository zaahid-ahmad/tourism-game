Fifty-three gates across all eight Grade 10 Tourism topics, five stages each. Standard mode: a wrong answer strands you and you retry that stage. Hardcore mode: one wrong answer wipes the whole run and you start again from Gate 01.
Achievements carry over between runs. "New run" lets you pick a mode again; "Wipe everything" also clears achievements. Answer order shuffles every attempt. Progress saves in this browser.
The leaderboard is optional and shares only a name you choose, plus your first-time-correct rate. Type board to view it or name <you> to set your name; type skip to stay off it. It runs on a best-effort basis and never affects your saved progress.
